# Edition 03: market evidence and curriculum decisions

Research and audit date: **4 November 2026**. The accompanying [evidence ledger](v3-market-evidence.json) records URLs, publication or update dates where established, retrieval dates, source types, short supporting excerpts, captured-content hashes, and limitations.

The evidence supports a curriculum built around problem definition, software, data quality, evaluation, and reliable delivery, followed by role-specific depth. It does **not** establish a universally best sequence, a time-to-employment estimate, or a representative measure of global junior hiring. The sequence and assessment design are authored educational judgments informed by the sources below.

## What was inspected

This refresh contains **nine primary-source entries**: six official report or documentation pages and three employer job-board feeds. From the feeds, **ten distinct role descriptions** were purposively selected across Anthropic, Elastic, and Datadog, with listed locations in India, the United Kingdom, the United States, Spain, and France. Geographic variants of the same principal role were not counted as independent skill observations.

The audit matched all nine stored source hashes against retained HTTPS response captures. All six report/documentation excerpts matched the captured text. All ten sampled job records matched their captured content hashes, titles, locations, update timestamps, and quoted passages. This verifies the evidence record against the retrieved material; it does not reproduce the underlying economic research. Raw pages and complete employer feeds remain research-working files rather than being redistributed in the roadmap archive.

The common dependency is observable: a model or API feature needs a defined decision; the decision needs valid inputs; the implementation needs tests; its claimed benefit needs a comparison; deployment introduces failures, costs, and changing conditions. That chain justifies learning the common software/data/evaluation trunk before specializing. It is stronger support for the roadmap than a list of currently fashionable frameworks.

## Entry access and global eligibility

The sample is deliberately informative about restrictions. Anthropic's inspected Applied AI Architect and Applied AI Engineer roles request 10+ and 4+ years respectively. The inspected pretraining role lists an advanced degree among its role qualifications. Elastic's principal AI role requests 10+ years; its marketing role requests relevant domain experience. These are evidence about the inspected descriptions, not automatic rejection rules for every role with those titles.

The Datadog Applied Science internship specifies an expected 2027 graduation and a six-month internship starting between February and June 2027. Its Madrid Software Engineering internship specifies expected 2027 graduation from a university in Spain and a February 2027 start. They are not unrestricted beginner openings. Employer descriptions also contain office or hybrid expectations; a country label, or a company's distributed-work language, does not establish that someone anywhere can work remotely for it.

For each possible application, record country, permitted remote locations, work authorization, degree/student status, graduation and start dates, required versus preferred experience, and the source passage. Mark a field unknown if the posting does not settle it. Assess eligibility separately from skill fit. No eligibility determination was made for the learner in this research.

## How the evidence changes edition 03

1. **Keep beginner work concrete.** The new foundation sequence builds one program across files, arithmetic, types, branches, loops, functions, and explicit failure handling. Time spent reading remains separate from evidence of understanding.
2. **Repeat evaluation throughout the curriculum.** Every module gains a transfer task, observable rubric, misconception repair, and explanation prompt. A critical failure such as leakage must not disappear inside an average score.
3. **Make production behavior visible.** Projects should document contracts, validation, authentication, retries, reproducibility, monitoring, and resource budgets where applicable. A demo succeeding once does not establish a dependable service.
4. **Preserve specialization.** Research mathematics and high-performance systems remain deliberate later branches. They should not overload a beginner's first day or be implied as prerequisites for every applied AI role.
5. **Record assistance honestly.** Working code produced with a solution or substantial hint is practice evidence. A fresh transfer or debugging task is needed to support a claim of independent understanding. The research does not establish a universal productivity effect for AI assistance.
6. **Review opportunities periodically.** Use the opportunity ledger to adjust an elective or portfolio gap. Do not rebuild the whole learning sequence in response to one job title or tool announcement.

## Future scenarios and refresh method

The future cannot be read directly from current job descriptions. If models and coding assistance improve, problem definition, evaluation, integration, and independent debugging remain useful investments. If cost, latency, or reliability constrains a product, measurement and systems work deserve greater attention. If a framework loses relevance, a project with explicit behavior and reproducible tests is easier to reimplement. These are conditional curriculum decisions, not forecasts of occupation counts or tool winners.

For a later market refresh, choose a clearly defined role family and geography, include employers beyond these three, record the sampling procedure, deduplicate location variants, and separate required from preferred qualifications. Preserve the original text date and retrieval date. If presenting frequencies, disclose the denominator and sampling limitations; do not describe a convenience sample as global prevalence. Revisit authoritative occupational projections on their own publication cycle and retain the population and horizon attached to every number.

The earlier edition's blocked-source notes remain a historical record of the October research conditions. This November refresh supersedes the claim that no job sample or direct labor-market sources could be retrieved; it does not retroactively change what was verified in editions 01 and 02.
